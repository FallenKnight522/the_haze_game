IPR STATEMENT RECORD
====================

Subject name: Mr Olsen
Date of experience: 2003-11-01
Statement subject: Vegetarians

Full statement:
This one is from my visit to Norway. I am a reporter, you see, focusing on investigative journalism. I traveled to Norway because of some financial scandal among the Norwegian elite. Anyway, you can read all about it in the news; it is not important here.

What is important is my visit to Mr. and Mrs. IPR2-THQ3_OP_BKBW, to talk about their standing in the affair. I visited them just for an interview, standard stuff, and they invited me to dinner afterwards. I obliged, since they were very insistent. But I warned them that I am a vegan. They said it was no trouble at all, that they would make sure the staff prepared something vegan. Although, come to think of it, maybe they said "someone," and I just misinterpreted it.

The dinner began. The first peculiar thing was the waiter. He looked like a five-year-old kid, addressed as IPR1-UO-MOYM. It was strange, but I decided not to comment on it. Even stranger was that he addressed Mr. and Mrs. IPR2-THQ3_OP_BKBW as "mum and dad". I thought it absurd that they would use their own child as their waiter. But, again, I chose not to mention it.

The main course arrived, and it was a meaty dish. Loads and loads of steaks, ribs, and large too. At first, I thought it must be from a boar, but no, the bones were too large for that. I asked Mr. IPR2-THQ3_OP_BKBW where the vegan food he promised was, and he simply smiled and pointed at the dish. "It is vegan, can't you see?" I froze. Then I tried to politely explain that vegan meant no meat, but he just looked confused. "It's vegan meat," he said. "What?" I responded in confusion. I was not sure if this was some sort of joke. But he looked serious. Before I could protest, Mrs. IPR2-THQ3_OP_BKBW got up and caringly said, "Don't worry, silly, look." She opened a large pot and fished out a human head. "Were you vegan?" she asked the head. "Yes," the severed head answered. "See?" said Mrs. IPR2-THQ3_OP_BKBW, and took a huge bite of the steak in front of her.

Needless to say, I ran. I tried to report the obvious murder, but no evidence was found by the Norwegian police. And so, I decided to make a record of it at your institute. I am not stupid; I know no one would believe me if I started spouting about talking heads and so on. But you should. And I need a record of this somewhere. In case I go missing as the next vegan meal of Mr. and Mrs. IPR2-THQ3_OP_BKBW.
