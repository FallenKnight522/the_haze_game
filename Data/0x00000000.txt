IPR STATEMENT RECORD
====================

Subject name: Nicola IPR1-G1-KBNAYJLUEVRVP
Date of experience: 2001-08-10
Statement subject: Thing that wasn't her son

Full statement:
Thank you for allowing me to make this statement. I understand that it wasn't easy, considering my husband is one of the IPR1-MO-ZSQGTTQYSA and he specifically forbade it... but I need there to be a record of what happened to us somewhere... and you are the only ones willing to listen to an old mad hag like me.

This all started in 1996. We had been a happy couple, IPR1-TI-JHQLFJ and I. This was before he became one of the world's leading researchers in the paranormal. In fact, it may very well have been the trigger. Our little boy was just five, and both of us loved him dearly. We lived in a small village near Semshlach, Austria. I do not remember how it all started. But I remember the first time I noticed it.

One night, when it was stormy outside, a little boy wandered into our bedroom. He said, "Mom, I am scared. Please, can I sleep with you and Dad?" Our little angel used to do that sometimes. At the time, I thought nothing of it. Only in the morning did I realize that was not my son. He too was five years old, or at least looked about five, and had similar hair, eyes. But it just wasn't him. I do not have any photos, so you have to take my word for it, but I am certain it was not my son. I told my husband, who slept through the whole ordeal, but by that time the boy had "gone to eat breakfast" and disappeared. At first, we thought it was a dream. But it kept happening.

Sometimes, he just came to us when our little angel was playing somewhere, pretending to be him and asking to leave. Sometimes, he pretended he got hurt while playing. Sometimes he appeared in our house. We tried everything. My husband worked for a security company, so cameras, fences, everything. It did not work. It was like it appeared out of thin air, invisible to any camera, or appearing on the record as our boy. We even checked whether the house was haunted, tried an exorcism, and confronted the thing. But nothing seemed to work.

And then, one day, it all went to hell. We had just about made our peace with the new inhabitant. One night, I was woken up by a strange noise from our son's room. I got up and went there. The room was a complete mess, like a fight had just broken out. In the middle, the thing that was not my son stood. "Sorry Mom, I just had a strange nightmare," it said. But there was no sign of my child. IPR1-TI-JHQLFJ stood right beside me, horrified. We searched everywhere, but our son was gone. The thing took his place. It looked similar, similar enough that all the photos of our son could be photos of it. But IPR1-TI-JHQLFJ and I both knew it was not our son. After two weeks of living with this pretender, we decided. We left it there and moved to Germany.

It did not stop the horrors, however. Sometimes, when I am in public, the thing runs to me, claiming to be my child, crying, and begging for me not to abandon it again. I usually manage to lose it before I go home, in the bustling city. But it tears my heart every time to look as if I am cold-heartedly abandoning a five-year-old child. But I know better. That thing is not my son. It killed him. It drove my husband to this life of constant danger. And if it does not stop, it might just drive me mad.

Post Statement notes by Jonah Sims: Nicola IPR1-G1-KBNAYJLUEVRVP did suffer a breakdown in the spring of 2002, after which she was hospitalized. She was then moved to a mental health institution, where she died of natural causes the following year. I did not notify IPR1-TI-JHQLFJ of this statement until her death. Though it led to quite the argument, in the end, he agreed with preserving it, and even added his own statement about the thing he dubbed "IPR1-99-KJYRY".
